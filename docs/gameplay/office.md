# Office and missions

The Office's Terminal and its screens, Transport offers and Contracts, and a mission from loading to payout. Flight is in [`flight.md`](flight.md); locations, the clock and scenery in [`world-and-scenery.md`](world-and-scenery.md). Terms are defined in [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md).

- **Controls:** in the Office, Left/Right (and Up/Down) move between the main screen's tiles, Left/Right browse the Shipyard's Ship offers or move between popup buttons, Up/Down move between the Hangar's berths or a Ship page's actions, Up/Down move between Transport offers and their ACCEPT buttons, or between Contracts, DESTINATION, START PERIOD and START LOADING, Right/Left move between the Transport offers screen's columns, Return opens, selects or confirms (or cycles DESTINATION's or START PERIOD's value), Escape counts as No in a popup, closes the Transport offer under the cursor, returns to the main screen from any other screen, and closes the Office from the main screen. Arrows, Return and Escape use Godot's built-in `ui_*` actions. Flight keys are in [`flight.md`](flight.md).
- **Office:** every fresh start opens on the Office, and so does Recovery after a crash (see `flight.md`): a full-window Terminal (black, green outlines and text in the Silkscreen pixel font, a border, scanlines) with the header `ORBITAL FREIGHT CO. // ` plus the screen's name, the balance at the header's right end on every screen (white, red below €0, `-€700`), and a key hint for the screen at the bottom (arrows in a hint are drawn, Silkscreen has no arrow glyphs). The game's version (e.g. `V0.1.0`) sits dimly at the right end of the key hint row.
  - **Main screen** (`// OFFICE`): every opening of the Office lands here. Four tiles in a row, SHIPYARD, HANGAR, TRANSPORT OFFERS and BANK ACCOUNT (a label too wide for its tile wraps onto two lines); the selected one is inverted, and movement stops at both ends. Return opens a tile; Escape on any other screen comes back here with the same tile selected. Escape here powers the Terminal off and leaves the ship in service Docked (SPARROW 1 at run start).
  - **Shipyard** opens on the Ship offer of the ship in service's type. It shows one Ship offer at a time for every Ship type, the Sparrow included: `HANGAR 1/4` (owned ships over berths) top left, a `< n/N >` indicator top right, a wireframe picture of the hull, the name with `OWNED: n` (owned ships of that type) beside it, the flavour line, PRICE (Sparrow €250 000, Mule €400 000, Halo €650 000; red when the balance can't pay it), TANK in litres with `FULL` (`6 000 L FULL`: the price includes a full tank; 0.8 kg to the litre), SEATS, CARGO (the full-Tank cargo with no passengers) and MAX CAPACITY (the maximum capacity) in kg, THRUST and HANDLING as 5-block bars, and a **BUY €x** button. Left/Right browse, wrapping at both ends. Return on BUY asks `BUY A MULE FOR €400 000?` (Yes selected; the price white). Yes takes the price from the balance as a Transaction, adds a new owned ship of that type to a free berth of the Hangar with a full tank and an automatic name (the type and a number counting up per type, never reused, even after a sale: `MULE 1`, `MULE 2`, and after selling MULE 1 the next is `MULE 3`), and opens its Ship page with a short white notice under the screen, `MULE 1 IS IN THE HANGAR`. A bought ship never goes into service by itself, so buying works with Contracts. Buying is blocked when the balance would drop below €0 (`NOT ENOUGH MONEY: €230 000 SHORT`, also while in debt) or the Hangar is full (`HANGAR FULL 4/4: SELL A SHIP FIRST`): BUY is then dimmed with that red reason under it, and Return on it only shows the reason as a red notice.
  - **Hangar** shows a table of berths headed `BERTHS 2/4`, with green column headings SHIP, CONDITION, FUEL, PARACHUTE and UPGRADES over a horizontal rule, then one row per owned ship in the order they were bought: its name (with `IN SERVICE`), its condition (`100%`), its fuel in litres, its parachute (`–` until parachutes exist) and its number of upgrades (`0`). The four berths always show: each empty one is a dim `EMPTY BERTH: BUY A SHIP IN THE SHIPYARD` row. Up/Down move (the selected row is inverted, stopping at both ends); Return on a ship opens its Ship page, Return on an empty berth opens the Shipyard. Only the ship in service is in the parking spot; the others live only on the Terminal.
  - **Ship page** (`// HANGAR // SPARROW 1`): on the left the ship's name, an `IN SERVICE` tag when it's the ship in service, its wireframe and its fitted upgrades (`NO UPGRADES` for now); in the middle CONDITION, FUEL (`3 200 / 6 000 L`), DRY MASS, CARGO (its full-Tank cargo) and VALUE (its Ship type's price); on the right a column of actions, Up/Down to move (the selected one inverted), Return to take it:
    - **PUT IN SERVICE** (the Ship swap) asks `PUT MULE 1 IN SERVICE?`; Yes puts it Docked in the parking spot at once, for free, with the fuel it was left with; the ship it replaces keeps its fuel in its berth (any fuel still being pumped into it goes in at once). The page stays open, now tagged IN SERVICE. Blocked with `ALREADY IN SERVICE` on the ship in service, `WRECK: REPAIR IT FIRST` on a Wreck, and `LOCKED: CONTRACTS ACCEPTED` while there are Contracts.
    - **FUEL** opens the ship's FUEL screen (`HANGAR // SPARROW 1 // FUEL`), for any owned ship but a Wreck (`WRECK: REPAIR IT FIRST`). On the left: the ship's name, `IN TANK 3 200 L (2 560 KG)` (the fuel in the tank now, growing or shrinking while it's pumped), `FUEL CAP 6 000 L (4 800 KG)` (min(Tank, Maximum weight − dry mass − Load), the Load aboard or loading counting for the ship in service), `PRICE €0.60 / L` and `TOTAL €600` (`YOU GET €600` when selling back). Then a vertical bar of the Tank: green up to the fuel in it, a dim pulsing green part for the fuel still to pump and the amount chosen (above the green when buying, the top of the green's place when selling back), and a white line at the fuel cap when it's below the Tank. Right of the bar, one control running from SELL BACK to BUY, `< BUY +1 000 L >` or `< SELL BACK -1 000 L >`: Left/Right step it to the next round 1 000 L, never past selling the whole tank or buying up to the fuel cap; it opens on +1 000 L (or as much as fits). Under it **FULL** sets it to fill up to the cap, then a **BUY FUEL** or **SELL FUEL** button (dimmed with `NO FUEL CHOSEN` at 0 L). Up/Down move between the three. Return on the button asks `BUY 1 000 L FOR €600?` or `SELL 1 000 L FOR €600?`; buying may take the balance below €0, with the red `THIS LEAVES YOU AT -€700` under the question. Yes pays at once, as one Transaction, and the fuel goes in (or out): at once for a ship in a berth, pumped at 500 l/s for the ship in service (see Fuel in [`flight.md`](flight.md)); fuel still being pumped already counts toward the cap. While fuel is pumped, the Hangar's table, the Ship page and IN TANK show it growing (or shrinking). The price per litre is the station's €0.60 × the Ship type's fuel multiplier (Sparrow ×1, Mule ×0.6, Halo ×1.3), rounded to the cent; the total is litres × that price, rounded to the euro, the same for buying and selling back. The Hangar is the only place that buys fuel back. Escape goes back to the Ship page.
    - **REPAIR €x** asks `REPAIR SPARROW 1 FOR €62 500?`; Yes restores Condition to 100% at once (a repair is always full) for missing Condition % × the Ship type's repair rate (Sparrow 25%, Mule 15%, Halo 30%) × the ship's value, as a Transaction (`REPAIR — SPARROW 1`). Allowed with Contracts and on credit: when it would end below €0, the confirm adds a red `THIS LEAVES YOU AT -€55 000`. Blocked with `NO DAMAGE` at 100%.
    - **SELL €x** asks `SELL MULE 1 FOR €200 000?`; Yes pays 50% of its value less its repair cost (never below €0; the fuel in its tank earns nothing) as a Transaction, frees its berth and returns to the Hangar's table. Allowed in debt; blocked with `THE SHIP IN SERVICE CAN'T BE SOLD` for the ship in service, so the player always owns a ship.

    A blocked action stays selectable but dimmed, with its red reason under it; Return on it only shows the reason as a red notice. Escape goes back to the Hangar's table.
  - **Transport offers** lists the Transport offers (new ones arrive only while fewer than 7 are listed; a removed Contract can take it past 7) in its left column and the Contracts in its right one. Each row shows its number (`#001`, numbered through the run), title, destination (`VALLEY`, `MINE` or `METROPOLIS`), PASSENGERS, CARGO or PASSENGERS + CARGO, and the time left to accept it as an in-game countdown, a started hour or minute counting as a whole one: hours (`20H` … `2H`), then in-game minutes for its last in-game hour, 30 real seconds (`60M` … `1M`); it's white, red in that last hour (black on the selected row, dimmed with the rest of an offer the ship can't take). Rows alternate between two faint green tints. Return on a row opens the offer's details under it (passengers, cargo, deadline as an in-game duration (`DEADLINE 3H 00M`), trader, value and destination (`DESTINATION MINE`), labels green and values white, with an ACCEPT button at their bottom right), on a slightly stronger tint, growing open over ~0.15 s (what isn't uncovered yet isn't drawn), and Return again closes them, shrinking them back the same way (the offer counts as closed at once: its ACCEPT is no longer a stop, and reopening grows the details from where they were); several offers can be open at once, and opening or closing one leaves the others. One cursor moves with Up/Down (stopping at both ends) through each offer's row, followed by its ACCEPT when it's open; the selected row or ACCEPT is inverted. Opening or closing an offer leaves the cursor on its row. When the list is taller than its area it scrolls just enough to keep the cursor's row or ACCEPT in view (on an open offer whose ACCEPT is dimmed, the row with its details, as much as fits), and small drawn ▲/▼ markers just right of the list's top and bottom show when more lies above or below; rows and lines that don't fit wholly in the area aren't drawn. The tints, animation time and marker size are constants in `office.gd` and `expandable_list.gd`. Escape on an open offer's row or ACCEPT closes that offer and puts the cursor on its row; anywhere else it returns to the main screen. An offer the ship can't take (counting the Contracts) has its text dimmed (row, reason and details; the cursor's highlight stays bright on it), with `OTHER DESTINATION` (while there are Contracts, it goes elsewhere than theirs), `NOT ENOUGH PASSENGER SEATS` or `OVERLOADED` (its cargo would pass the maximum capacity) under its row, the first that applies, and its ACCEPT is dimmed and skipped by the cursor; fuel never disables an offer. An offer that fits but whose Load would, on top of the Contracts, push the fuel aboard over the fuel cap stays lit and acceptable, with a red `SELLS 400 L FUEL` note under its row (the fuel it adds to what START LOADING sells back); one that would only leave room for less than a full Tank gets a red `FUEL LIMITED` note instead. Return on an ACCEPT asks "ARE YOU SURE?" (Yes selected) for that offer only; Yes makes that offer a Contract, which leaves the list (nothing takes its place at once). Confirming checks again that the offer is still there and still fits, its destination included. An open offer that expires just closes; if its ARE YOU SURE? was showing, that closes too, nothing is accepted and `OFFER EXPIRED` shows at the bottom left for 2 s. The right column lists CONTRACTS (`#006 TITLE`), then `PASSENGERS X/Y`, `CARGO X/Y KG` (accepted cargo over the full-Tank cargo for the accepted passengers), `FUEL LIMITED: X/Y L` when the Contracts' Load leaves room for less than a full Tank (the fuel cap over the Tank), `DELIVER WITHIN 2H 30M` (the shortest Deadline among the Contracts, as an in-game duration: 2 in-game minutes per real second) and `TOTAL VALUE €N` (the sum of their rewards), both hidden while there are no Contracts, then the mission rows, `DESTINATION VALLEY` and `START PERIOD EVENING` (green labels, white values; a whole row inverted while it holds the cursor), and a START LOADING button; Return on it starts loading (see Loading). Without Contracts it reads START instead and starts a free flight (see Free flight). DESTINATION shows a location; Return on it cycles Valley → Mine → Metropolis → Valley. Accepting the first Contract sets it to that Contract's destination and locks it: while there are Contracts, Return on it does nothing. Removing the last Contract unlocks it, keeping its value. START PERIOD shows Morning, Day, Evening or Night; Return on it cycles Morning → Day → Evening → Night → Morning. Each time the Office opens, they start on the Contracts' destination (else the current location) and the start period holding the current local time. They work without Contracts too, but only START LOADING (or START) applies them: changing them without loading leaves the location and the local time alone. Summary labels are green and values white; once the cargo passes the full-Tank cargo the CARGO value turns red, and FUEL LIMITED shows, all red, at exactly that moment. Cargo beyond the full-Tank cargo, up to the maximum capacity, can still be accepted. Right moves focus to the Contracts column, on START LOADING (open offers stay open; the list's row is then outlined), Left moves it back. There, Up/Down move through the Contracts, then DESTINATION, then START PERIOD, then START LOADING (stopping at both ends); each Contract has a plain `X` at the right end of its row, on the same baseline as its text, and the selected Contract's whole row is inverted, and START LOADING is inverted only while it holds the cursor. Return on a Contract asks "REMOVE CONTRACT?" (No selected); No or Escape keeps it, Yes removes it: no payout, no transaction, its seats and cargo are freed, and it goes back to the offers with the same number, in number order (closed), with the time left it had when accepted (an offer's time doesn't run while it's a Contract), even when 7 or more are listed; it keeps its destination. The cursor then stays on a Contract while any are left, otherwise on START LOADING. Contracts stay accepted when offers expire and when the player leaves the Office; once loading starts they can't be removed (Return on one does nothing). During a loaded mission (from the end of loading until unloading starts) every offer is dimmed with `MISSION UNDER WAY` and can't be accepted, and the button reads CANCEL MISSION: Return on it asks "CANCEL MISSION?" (No selected); No or Escape keeps the mission, Yes cancels it (see Cancelling) and the cursor stays on the button, which then reads START.

  Transport offers come from the Economy. A run starts with 5. While fewer than 7 are listed, a new one arrives after a random 30–90 s (the wait pauses while 7 are listed and is drawn afresh when it runs again). Each one expires 180–600 s after it appears (offer timers run everywhere, Office open or not). Offers leave the pool only by expiring or being accepted, and neither brings a new one at once. Each offer's destination is drawn first, by the locations' pool shares (Valley 40%, Mine 30%, Metropolis 30%; fixed when it appears), and the rest comes from that destination's location profile: its kind weights (passengers only / cargo only / mixed), passenger and cargo ranges (in 100 kg steps, each capped by the largest ship type's seats and maximum capacity, so some won't fit a smaller ship; nothing makes sure one fits), titles and reward factor. The Valley: ⅓ / ⅓ / ⅓, 1–10 passengers, 500 kg to the maximum, ×1.0, with varied passengers, tourists, luggage, supplies and cargo. The Mine: 10% / 60% / 30%, 1–4 passengers, 3 000 kg to the maximum, ×1.6, with tools, parts, industrial supplies and small crews. The Metropolis: 60% / 10% / 30%, 1–10 passengers, 500–3 000 kg, ×1.35, mostly tourists and corporate workers, with occasional light cargo, mail and shop supplies. Deadline 60–180 s everywhere; reward €15 000 per passenger and €6 000 per 1 000 kg of cargo at a 120 s deadline, × (120 / deadline)^0.5, × a random 0.7–1.3, × the destination's reward factor. On average a Mine offer pays about a fifth more than a Valley one (heavy and cargo-led, so it needs the larger factor), and a Metropolis offer a little less than a Mine one (about €91 000, €110 000 and €108 500). The pool sizes and arrival interval live in `economy.gd`, the location profiles in `location.gd`, the other numbers in `transport_offer.gd`.
  - **Bank account** shows `PILOT` and `BALANCE €20 000` (label green, amount white): the balance in EUR, held by the Economy node and starting at €20 000 every Career (`START_BALANCE` in `economy.gd`; a negative one reads `-€12 000`: Recovery, Repair and fuel may go into debt, buying a ship may not). Every amount of money is written with its thousands split by spaces, sign first (`-€1 234 567`), then, a row's height below, one transaction per delivered, cancelled or Failed contract, per ship bought or sold, per recovery fee, per repair and per fuel bought or sold back, newest first, its row showing only the payout: `#004 INDUSTRIAL SUPPLIES  CARGO  €54 400`, `SHIP PURCHASE — MULE 1  SHIP  -€400 000`, `SHIP SALE — MULE 1  SHIP  €200 000`, `RECOVERY — SPARROW 1  SHIP  -€12 500`, `REPAIR — SPARROW 1  SHIP  -€62 500`, `FUEL  STATION  2 000 L  SHIP  -€1 200` (a sell-back reads the same with a positive amount); the amount is white for a gain (or €0) and red for a loss, the rest of the row green (all black on the selected row). A Contract that paid €0 still gets one. The transactions work like the Transport offer list (`ExpandableList`), without buttons: alternating row tints, Up/Down move the cursor (stopping at both ends, the selected row inverted), Return opens or closes the transaction's details on the stronger tint with the same opening and closing animation, several can be open at once, the list scrolls to keep the cursor's row in view, with its details when it's open (as much as fits) with ▲/▼ markers, and Escape on an open transaction closes it (anywhere else it returns to the main screen). Open details show, labels green and values white: `BASE VALUE €64 000`, `PENALTY -€9 600 (24 MIN OVERDUE)` (Silkscreen has no `−` glyph, so it's a hyphen; the whole real seconds overdue the euros were counted for, as in-game minutes: 2 per second) or `PENALTY €0` when delivered on time, `PAYOUT €54 400`, `DEADLINE 14:30` (the flight's deadline the penalty was counted against, as a local time: the shortest among its Contracts, which can be shorter than this Contract's own), `LANDED 14:54` (the local time of landing on the pad), `DELIVERED TO VALLEY` (the location landed at) and `SHIP SPARROW` (the ship type in service when it was settled). A cancelled Contract (see Cancelling) shows `BASE VALUE €42 000`, `CANCELLATION FEE -€10 500`, `PAYOUT -€10 500`, `CANCELLED` and `SHIP SPARROW`, without the deadline, landing and delivered-to lines. A Contract unloaded on a pad that isn't its destination (see Payout) shows `WRONG DESTINATION -€64 000` in place of the PENALTY line, `PAYOUT €0`, and `DESTINATION MINE` after DELIVERED TO. Times on the day after loading finished end in ` +1` (`LANDED 00:15 +1`). A Failed contract (lost in a crash) shows `BASE VALUE €42 000`, `FAILURE FEE -€21 000`, `PAYOUT -€21 000`, `FAILED` and `SHIP SPARROW`. A recovery fee opens to `SHIP SPARROW 1`, `SHIP TYPE PRICE €250 000`, `RECOVERY FEE 5%` and `PAYOUT -€12 500`; a repair to `SHIP SPARROW 1`, `REPAIRED 100%`, `REPAIR RATE 25%`, `VALUE €250 000` and `PAYOUT -€62 500`; fuel to `SHIP SPARROW 1`, `FUEL 2 000 L (1 600 KG)`, `PRICE €0.60 / L`, `SHIP TYPE FACTOR ×0.6` (only for a Mule's or a Halo's), `PLACE STATION` and `PAYOUT -€1 200`. A ship purchase opens to `SHIP MULE 1`, `PRICE €400 000` and `PAYOUT -€400 000`; a sale to `SHIP MULE 1`, `VALUE €400 000`, `SALE SHARE 50%`, `REPAIR COST €0` and `PAYOUT €200 000`.

  The world is paused while the Office is showing. The Terminal powers on with a ~0.3 s CRT effect (a dot stretches into a bright line, which opens into the picture) and powers off with the reverse; the flight view shows only once power-off is over, and Office input is ignored during the effect. The "ENTER THE OFFICE?" popup over the flight view opens and closes without it, and so do the Office's own popups, the notice line and moving between screens.
- **Loading:** START LOADING powers the Terminal off and starts loading over the flight view, starting the mission: the location becomes the chosen destination, the Contracts' (its pad and terrain replace the old ones while the ship is docked at the station, and the HUD shows its name, `MINE  14:07`), and the local time is set to a random whole minute within the chosen start period (Morning 06:00–11:59, Day 12:00–17:59, Evening 18:00–23:59, Night 00:00–05:59), re-rolled every time, even for the current period. The clock keeps running from there through loading, the delivery flight and unloading. Fuel above the fuel cap for the Contracts' Load is sold back at the station price, as one positive FUEL Transaction, and pumped out at 500 l/s while loading (`Pumping fuel… 2s` under the countdown; the ship stays put until both are done). Loading never adds fuel. The ship stays Docked and held (Up doesn't undock, Left/Right don't turn it, no cancelling) while the message counts down `Loading… 7s`; loading takes 3 s + 1 s per passenger + 1 s per 1 000 kg of cargo. When it finishes, the Load is added to the ship's mass, the hold is released, and the message shows `Loaded! Deliver by 14:30` (the flight deadline as a local time, see HUD in [`flight.md`](flight.md)), plus `Fuel limited to 4 600 l (3 680 kg)` when the cap is below a full Tank, until the ship undocks. The delivery clock starts the moment loading finishes, even if the ship stays Docked; its Deadline is the shortest among the Contracts. Docking again while loaded asks "ENTER THE OFFICE?" as usual, so the mission can be cancelled; no Contracts can be added.
- **Cancelling:** CANCEL MISSION in the Office, during a loaded mission, gives up every Contract at once. Each one is charged a cancellation fee of 25% of its reward (rounded), recorded as a transaction with a negative payout (`-€10 500`) and taken from the balance, which may go negative. The Load comes off at once (the fuel cap is a full Tank again), the mission ends (no HUD deadline, no delivery clock), and accepting offers and ship swap unlock. Cancelled Contracts don't go back to the Transport offers.
- **Free flight:** with no Contracts the button reads START. It powers the Terminal off and starts the mission like START LOADING (the location becomes the chosen destination, the local time a random whole minute within the chosen start period), but nothing is loaded: the ship stays Docked and is released, so Up undocks it at once, there's no fuel cap, no deadline in the HUD and no delivery clock. Landing on any pad is a plain Landed with no unloading, and docking again asks "ENTER THE OFFICE?" as usual.
- **Unloading:** becoming Landed on the current location's pad while delivering (a real landing or the debug pad shortcut) starts unloading by itself, whether or not it's the Contracts' destination (drift may have taken that away, see Location drift in [`world-and-scenery.md`](world-and-scenery.md)); the delivery clock stops and the HUD deadline goes. The message counts down `Unloading… 4s` (2 s + 0.5 s per passenger + 0.5 s per 1 000 kg of cargo). Lift-off is blocked (Up does nothing), but refuelling goes on as usual, up to the fuel cap while the Load is aboard. Only the first landing counts: a ship that settles back, or lands again, doesn't unload twice.
- **Payout:** when unloading finishes, each Contract is settled on its own for the whole seconds overdue at landing (rounded down, like the red counter): it loses 2% of its passenger reward and 1% of its cargo reward per second, each part never below €0. Unloaded on a pad that isn't its destination, it pays €0 instead (its whole reward is lost, however late). The total goes into the Bank account, one transaction per Contract is added, and a small green `+€42 000` rises above the ship and fades over 2 s. The Load and the Contracts are cleared, the fuel cap is a full Tank again (refuelling carries on), and the ship can lift off.
- **Ship swap:** every time the ship docks (not at a fresh start or Recovery, not on the pad, not while loading; while delivering too), a popup over the flight view asks "ENTER THE OFFICE?" with No selected, once the ship has finished easing upright (undocking before then cancels it). From the moment the popup shows until the ship is released it's frozen: Up doesn't undock and Left/Right don't turn it; R does nothing. No or Escape closes the popup and leaves the ship free to undock. Yes opens the Office on the main screen. PUT IN SERVICE on another owned ship's Ship page puts it Docked in the same parking spot; the station isn't rebuilt. While there are Contracts, ship swapping is locked (`LOCKED: CONTRACTS ACCEPTED`), but buying isn't. Leaving the Office (Escape on the main screen) releases the ship as No would, whether or not it was swapped.
- **Wreck in service:** while the ship in service is a Wreck, the main screen shows its name with a red `WRECKED` tag under the tiles, the Hangar's CONDITION reads `WRECKED` in red, and the Office can't be left: Escape on the main screen and START or START LOADING (dimmed) only show the red notice `WRECK IN SERVICE: REPAIR IT OR PUT ANOTHER SHIP IN SERVICE`.
