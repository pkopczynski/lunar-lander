# One planet clock with drifting locations, not a fresh clock per mission

Local time is a single clock that always runs while the office isn't showing, so the planet seems to turn under the station. It doesn't stop between missions or reset per destination. Starting loading moves it to the destination and re-rolls the time within the chosen start period. After two whole days in deep space the location moves on to the next one, even during a delivery, and landing on whichever pad is below delivers. We picked this over the first spec's "a clock per mission, none outside it" because a clock that vanishes between missions breaks the feeling of a living planet. It also gives the sky (and later weather) one time source that always exists. The clock is shared: locations have no time zones and keep no time of their own, so a change of location never makes the time jump.
