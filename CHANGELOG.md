## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)

The first release: fly a freighter between an orbital station and the surface.

### Features

* **The Office:** every run opens on a green-on-black Terminal offering three ship types: the light Sparrow, the ring-shaped Halo and the heavy Mule, each with its own Tank, Maximum weight, thrust and handling. The Terminal powers on and off with a CRT effect.
* **Station and surface:** start Docked in the station's parking spot, fly out of the maze, cross the border from gravity-free deep space, and land on the pad, then fly back up and dock again.
* **Fuel and weight:** thrust burns fuel, and a heavier ship climbs slower and burns more. Refuel after landing on the pad or docking at the station.
* **Ship swap:** every time you dock, you can trade your ship for another ship type.
* **Backdrop:** two Tatra-like ridges behind the surface, with Rysy and Giewont's sleeping knight.
* **Version:** the game's version shows on the Terminal ([#12](https://github.com/pkopczynski/lunar-lander/issues/12)).
